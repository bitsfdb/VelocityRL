Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

VelocityRL values your privacy. We believe software should operate transparently without collecting personal information.

Data Collection

VelocityRL runs locally on your computer. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, account credentials, or user activity.

Local Storage

Your settings and configurations are stored solely on your local device. This data is never transmitted to external servers.

Network Requests

The application may connect to public endpoints strictly to retrieve public catalog information or check for software updates. These requests do not contain personal identifiers or telemetry.

Third-Party Services

We do not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics, or advertising SDKs. We do not sell or monetize user data under any circumstances.

Contact

Questions? Reach us on Discord or visit GitHub.
