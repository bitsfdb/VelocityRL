Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

VelocityRL ("we", "our", or "the app") is a desktop application for swapping Rocket League in-game item assets. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

VelocityRL is designed to operate primarily on your local machine. We may collect the following:

  • Diagnostic data (optional): If you agree, the app may send error reports and crash diagnostics to help us improve stability. This may include error messages, stack traces, and app version information.
  • Configuration data: Settings such as your game directory path and privacy preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Item database requests: The app may fetch item data from our API (api.velocityrl.tech) to populate the item search database. These requests do not include personal account information.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your Rocket League account credentials.
  • We do not access or transmit your game files except as required for local swap operations on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Use Information

Any diagnostic data we receive is used solely to:

  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Understand which features are most used

Local Processing

Asset swaps are performed entirely on your local machine. Modified game files and automatic backups remain on your device. VelocityRL does not upload your game files to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with the following third-party services:

  • GitHub: For release notes and update checks
  • api.velocityrl.tech: For the item database and optional diagnostics

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

Your Choices

When you first launch VelocityRL, you are asked to agree to this Privacy Policy. You can decline diagnostic data collection by not agreeing, though some features may require agreement to function. You may uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored configuration.

Data Retention

Diagnostic reports, if submitted, are retained only as long as necessary to investigate and resolve issues. Local configuration and backup files remain on your device until you delete them.

Children's Privacy

VelocityRL is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If changes are material, the app will prompt you to review and agree to the updated policy before continuing.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out via our Discord support server or open an issue on GitHub.
